## How to use it:

The files of this archive can simply be placed in the folder of the latex-presentation to do.
Alternatively they can be installed in the local tex-tree.

This template contains 6 predefined designs and is optimised for slide formats 4:3, 16:9, and 16:10. Slides can be created in German or English.
Each of the 6 designs is associated with an individual colour scheme for the subsequent slides. The title pages and an impression of the colour scheme can be viewed in the overview document (styleoverview-themeUniMS.pdf).
The themes and language can be specified as an option when loading the theme; the format as a documentclass-option.
